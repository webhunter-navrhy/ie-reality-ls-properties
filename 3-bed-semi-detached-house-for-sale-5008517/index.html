Description

59 Crann Nua, Portarlington, Co. Laois is an attractive three-bedroom semi-detached residence presented in very good condition throughout and offering spacious, well-appointed accommodation ideal for modern family living. Situated at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac within this popular residential development, the property enjoys a peaceful setting while remaining conveniently close to all local amenities.

The accommodation is thoughtfully laid out and comprises a welcoming entrance hall, a comfortable sitting room, a bright and functional kitchen, a versatile home office, and a guest WC on the ground floor. Upstairs, there are three generously proportioned bedrooms, all benefiting from built-in wardrobes, including a master bedroom with ensuite bathroom. A well-appointed family bathroom completes the first-floor accommodation.

The property is further enhanced by gas-fired central heating, parking, and excellent outdoor space. The rear garden features a substantial 12.5 sq. m garden shed with power supply, making it ideal for a home gym, workshop, hobby room, remote working space, or additional storage. Practical features include outdoor power sockets to both the front and rear gardens, together with an outside tap in the rear garden.

Crann Nua is a highly regarded residential development conveniently located within walking distance of Portarlington town centre, supermarkets, and the local secondary school. Primary schools and Portarlington Train Station are located approximately 3 km away, providing excellent connectivity for commuters and families alike.

This superb home combines comfort, convenience, and a desirable location, making it an ideal choice for first-time buyers, growing families, or those seeking a well-maintained property in a mature residential setting.
